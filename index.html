THE TRAVEL LING IMAGE

Every time an image is saved, compressed, forwarded, or screenshotted something changes. It may look roughly the same — but it carries new marks of travel. Watch that process happen in real time, then examine what it means.

Da Vinci — Mona Lisa — c.1503
Benjamin
1935

Every artwork carries an aura tied to its unique presence in time and place. Mechanical reproduction spreads images everywhere but hollows out that presence. We gain access; we lose singularity.

Steyerl
2009

The poor image is low-resolution, compressed, blurry, often pirated. As it circulates it loses visual quality but gains speed, reach, and social energy. It is poor in data and rich in life.

Question 01

Looking at the final state of the image, what has it visually lost? Be specific about what you can no longer read or identify.

Question 02

Steyerl says the poor image transforms quality into accessibility. What does she mean? Do you agree that this is a worthwhile exchange?

Question 03

Benjamin says reproduction weakens an image's aura. Did the aura of the Mona Lisa change as it circulated here? What would he say about WeChat specifically?

Question 04

Think of a real image you have seen travel across platforms — a meme, a news photo, a biaoqingbao. What changed about it as it moved? Was anything gained?
